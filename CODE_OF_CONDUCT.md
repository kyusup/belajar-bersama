# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Belajar Bersama a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, religion, or similar characteristics.

This is a learning community. Treat learners, contributors, checkers, and maintainers with respect.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Focusing on what is best for learners and educational quality
- Accepting constructive feedback
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing others’ private information (doxxing)
- Submitting or demanding educational content that you do not have the right to share

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. They may remove comments, commits, code, or other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

For this pre-production repository, report conduct issues privately using the same path as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when a public issue would cause harm.
