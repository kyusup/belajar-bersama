# Copyright and Content License — Belajar Bersama

Software licensing and educational-content licensing are separate concepts.

---

## 1. Software License

Recommended source-code license: **Apache License 2.0**.

This license applies to project software/code artifacts, not automatically to every educational resource hosted or linked by the platform.

---

## 2. Educational Content License

Recommended default for contributor-original educational content, where the contributor has the right to license it that way: **CC BY-SA**.

Important constraints:

- Do not assume every submitted work can be relicensed.
- Third-party excerpts, linked resources, publisher materials, and similarly restricted works may require different license/terms representation.
- Verified contributor status does **not** imply the material is freely reusable.

---

## 3. Required Representable Metadata

The content domain must be able to represent:

| Concept | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Author | Who created the educational material |
| Source | Origin of material when not solely original |
| Reference | Citations / supporting references |
| Attribution | Credit text/requirements |
| License | License or terms identifier/statement |
| External resource | Linked off-platform resource |
| Copyright / takedown status | Rights dispute or removal state |

Exact mandatory fields per content type at publish time: partially `OPEN DECISION` (minimum viable set still required for review readiness).

---

## 4. Contributor Responsibility

Contributors are responsible for ensuring they have the rights needed to submit material under the declared license/terms, and for providing accurate attribution/source information.

The platform’s review process should check readiness of these declarations, but review does not transfer legal responsibility away from the contributor.

---

## 5. Reporting Infringement

The platform must eventually support reporting potentially infringing content.

MVP does not require a full legal/takedown operations system.  
The domain must still allow:

- content reports related to copyright
- status reflecting disputed/removed material later
- audit of moderation/takedown-related actions later

---

## 6. Non-Assumptions

Do not assume:

- public visibility equals public domain
- open-source software license covers educational content
- CC BY-SA applies to third-party resources by default
- checker approval validates legal ownership beyond process checks
