# Product Principles — Belajar Bersama

Operational product principles derived from the Product Constitution.  
These guide day-to-day design and implementation choices.

---

## 1. Learning Access

- Public educational content is readable without login.
- Login unlocks personalization and contribution, not basic access to public learning.
- Barriers to learning (account walls, paywalls, unnecessary friction) are treated as product defects unless an explicit constitution amendment says otherwise.

---

## 2. Quality over Volume

- Prefer fewer high-quality, reviewed materials over large unreviewed dumps.
- Verification, review, taxonomy governance, and attribution are quality controls, not bureaucracy for its own sake.
- Content that cannot meet quality, copyright, or safety expectations should not be published.

---

## 3. Independent Learners

- Design learning flows that help users understand, practice, and progress on their own.
- Q&A supports learning, not social networking.
- Future AI assistants may explain or guide, but must not replace curated knowledge as the source of truth.

---

## 4. Formal and Informal Education Together

Support both:

- Formal pathways (for example SD, SMP, SMA, SMK, Perguruan Tinggi)
- Informal/professional pathways (for example programming, business, agriculture, life skills, hobbies)

Do not force all content into a school-grade model. Taxonomy must remain flexible and governed.

---

## 5. Contribution Is Earned and Scoped

- Authentication alone does not grant publishing rights.
- Verification is competency-scoped.
- Contributors create drafts and submit for review; they do not self-publish educational materials.
- Contribution scope is limited to competencies for which the user is verified.

---

## 6. Review Before Publication

- Publication requires eligible checker review.
- Makers normally revise based on feedback; checkers should not silently rewrite maker work as the default path.
- Auditability of review decisions is required.

---

## 7. Language

- Initial UI and primary content language: Indonesian.
- Lessons teaching another language may use that language.
- Keep strings and content metadata structured so localization remains feasible later.

---

## 8. Privacy and Minors

- Privacy by default.
- Do not expose emails, auth details, or unnecessary personal data in public profiles or APIs.
- No private direct messaging in MVP.
- Collect the minimum identity needed for authentication, contribution, and safety/moderation.

---

## 9. Copyright Respect

- Verified contribution does not imply free reuse rights.
- Represent author, source, attribution, license, and takedown/report status in the domain.
- Report paths for potentially infringing content must remain possible.

---

## 10. Content Safety (Future-ready, MVP-light)

Sensitive topics (health, law, finance, safety, and similar) may need warnings, stronger review, or source requirements later.  
MVP must not over-engineer this, but must not make it impossible.

---

## 11. Open Source and Sustainability

- Software is open source.
- Educational content licensing is separate from software licensing.
- MVP remains free of monetization features.
- Donation/community support is a possible future sustainability path, not an MVP feature set.

---

## 12. Non-Goals (MVP)

Do not treat the following as MVP requirements:

- AI tutoring / AI as educational truth
- Adaptive learning engine
- Username/password auth
- Private messaging
- Paywalls, ads, subscriptions
- Full legal/takedown workflow UI
- Uncontrolled contributor-created taxonomies
